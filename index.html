        Setup

        With a key you get traffic-aware drive times and address search. Without one, presets still work.

        Strava — optional

        Create an app at strava.com/settings/api and set its Authorization Callback Domain to . Then paste its Client ID and Client Secret here.

        Mood playlists

        Assign your own playlists to a mood. Only playlists you own or collaborate on can be read.

        Swipe a playlist left to remove it from this list. It stays in Spotify. Playlists this app made are left out automatically.

        Connect Spotify to see your playlists.

        Favorites

        Use ▲ ▼ to set the order on the home screen, ✕ to remove. Rename a favorite, or search to change its address. Changes apply when you tap Save.

        Back up & restore

        Back up copies your Client ID, Google key, playlist moods and favorites as one block of text. Paste it into Notes, then into another copy of the app to restore. It includes your Google key and Strava secret, so keep it private. Spotify sign-in and loaded songs aren't included: after restoring, tap Connect Spotify, then Refresh playlists & songs.
